Intelligent and reconfigurable wireless communications

I am an Associate Lecturer (ECAF) and Research Associate in the School of Electrical Engineering and Telecommunications at the University of New South Wales, Sydney, Australia.

My research focuses on intelligent and reconfigurable wireless communication systems, including pinching-antenna systems, integrated sensing and communication, six-dimensional movable antenna systems, reconfigurable intelligent surfaces, and satellite-terrestrial networks. I develop communication-theoretic models, optimization algorithms, and learning-based methods for reliable, secure, and energy-efficient wireless systems.

Research

My research combines wireless communication theory, mathematical optimization, and learning-based methods to design future wireless systems.

Integrated Sensing and Communication

Joint design of communication, sensing, security, and resource allocation for next-generation wireless networks.

Reconfigurable Wireless Systems

Intelligent reflecting surfaces, pinching antennas, six-dimensional movable antennas, and other platforms that introduce controllable spatial degrees of freedom.

Satellite-Terrestrial Networks

Resource allocation, channel knowledge maps, and learning-assisted design for satellite-terrestrial and integrated networks.
